🖥️🚀 We released [**Sage S2.1 Flash**](https://internlm.github.io/Sage-S2.1-Flash/), a **320B MoE multimodal model** with strong **computer-use agent (CUA) capabilities** — understanding interfaces, operating desktop and web applications, and adapting actions through visual feedback. Our evaluations report **85.2 on OSWorld-Verified** and **67.6 on WebArena-Verified**. It also operates scientific software such as **3D Slicer and QGIS**, carrying complex research workflows through to verifiable results.
